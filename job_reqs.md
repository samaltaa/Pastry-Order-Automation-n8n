The role

The AI Automation Specialist builds the systems that run the agency. You will design, build and maintain automation workflows in n8n and Make, create chat and voice AI agents, and write the small backend services that connect everything. You take a process described in plain language, from client onboarding to support and reporting, and turn it into a working, documented system that the team depends on every day.

You will own the stack end to end: choosing models by cost and quality, self-hosting on a VPS with Docker, keeping data in Supabase or PostgreSQL, and making sure it all keeps running. The role can start part-time or project-based and grow with the work. What matters is shipped systems, not demos.
